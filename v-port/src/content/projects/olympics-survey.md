Python, Pandas, Streamlit, Plotly, Seaborn, Matplotlib

- Built an interactive Olympics data analysis dashboard in Python (Streamlit) covering 120 years of historical athlete and medal data.
- Analyzed medal tallies by year and country, participation trends across nations,sports and events over time, and athlete demographics like age, height/weight by sport, medal type.
- Used pandas for data preprocessing, Plotly, Seaborn and Matplotlib for interactive visualizations like line charts, heatmaps, distribution plots, including country wise medal trends and men vs women participation over time.
