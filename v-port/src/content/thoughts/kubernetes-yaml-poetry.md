date: "2025-11-06"
excerpt: "No syntax highlighting saves you from a semantic typo."
---

Two spaces here, a list under a key there, an entire deployment silently doing nothing because a `selector` didn't match a `label` by exactly one character. No syntax highlighting saves you from a semantic typo. I have stared at a `values.yaml` file the way people used to stare at unrhymed verse, searching for meaning that was, it turns out, just a missing colon.
