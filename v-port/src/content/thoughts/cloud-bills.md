---
title: "Cloud bills are just modern horror stories"
date: "2025-06-30"
excerpt: "Forgot to tear down a test cluster. The invoice arrived like a jump scare."
---

Left a small Kubernetes cluster running "just for a day" to test something, then got pulled into three other things and forgot about it for two and a half weeks. The bill, when it arrived, was less "unexpected expense" and more "plot twist." Nothing dramatic — no data loss, no outage, no incident channel — just a number that made me sit very still for a second.

New rule, immediately violated at least once so far: everything spun up for testing gets a calendar reminder before it gets a config file.
