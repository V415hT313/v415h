Renamed the same boolean four times today: `isValid`, then `isFormValid`, then `canSubmit`, then back to `isValid`, because the other two implied things it didn't actually check. Nothing was resolved. The variable behaves exactly the same regardless of what I call it. I just wanted it to stop looking at me like that.
